Applies to the enhanced planner. System follows your device setting.

What it adds

  • Drag a class anywhere in your plan, or send it to the top in one click. MyUCLA only moves one step per click.
  • Rearrange as much as you like first. Nothing reaches MyUCLA until you press Save.
  • Every class shows what it clashes with, without opening a popup for each one.
  • Add your own notes to a class. They stay on this computer.
Tidy up MyUCLA's own layout

Off by default. Browse classes beside your weekly schedule. Drag tabs together to group them, toward an edge to split, or outside to float. The shaded preview shows the destination; Escape cancels.

Your tab groups, closed panels and sizes are saved on this computer. Reopen panels from navigation, or choose Default layout to reset. Details can stay open for several classes. Information & help and Original layout remain available. UCLA navigation and native enrollment controls stay intact.

Stay signed in while reading

MyUCLA only treats a click as proof you are still there. Scrolling and reading do not count, so a long look at your plan can sign you out in the middle of editing. Turn this on and scrolling or moving the mouse counts too.

It never runs in a background tab, and it stops the moment you leave the page.

Stop extending after

MyUCLA also has a hard limit of about 4 hours. Nothing can push past that — you will have to sign in again.
